About Me

I'm a software engineer at Apptronik, where I deploy reinforcement learning on humanoid robots.
Previously, I received an MS in Electrical Engineering from Virginia Tech, where I studied the intersection of control theory and deep reinforcement learning at the Terrestrial Robotics and Controls Lab.
Before that, I interned at Shield AI, where I built RL systems to control and coordinate things that fly.
For my undergraduate studies, I majored in Controls, Robotics & Autonomy with a degree in Computer Engineering.
